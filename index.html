How it works

A clearer read, in three passes.

Designed around a simple idea: the score opens the conversation, the breakdown helps you decide what to do next.

No dashboard gymnastics. No verdicts dressed up as certainty.

01

Paste the draft

Begin with the text you are about to publish. Keep it as it is; the readout is about the draft, not the final polish.

02

Read the signals

Look for clusters, not single scores. Rhythm, repetition, and overlap each tell a different part of the story.

03

Revise with intent

Keep your voice. Use the breakdown to decide which passages deserve a second look before they go out.
